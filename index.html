Empowering Learners in Kenya with 21st Century Skills

Ubunifu works with Private Schools, Public Schools and Compassion Projects to strengthen performance in Mathematics and Science through competitions, mentorship, science congress, innovation challenges and practical learning experiences.

Our Key Programs

Our programs operate under the Akili Quest Initiative (AQI), a learning and mentorship platform dedicated to empowering learners in Kenya through academic excellence and practical skill development. Through AQI, we nurture innovation, leadership, teamwork, confidence, creativity and strong problem-solving abilities to prepare learners for real-world success and future careers.

Mathematics Contests

We organize exciting math competitions that sharpen learners’ speed, accuracy, confidence and love for Mathematics.

Science Congress

Learners showcase experiments and innovations through scientific research projects that build curiosity, creativity and analytical thinking.

21st Century Skills Training

We equip learners with communication, teamwork, leadership, creativity and digital skills required for success in modern society.

✔ Trusted by Schools

✔ Practical Learning Approach

We focus on hands-on learning, making science and math enjoyable and understandable through competitions and projects.

✔ Innovation & Creativity

Ubunifu encourages learners to think beyond the classroom by developing creativity, innovation and real-world problem-solving skills.

✔ Mentorship & Guidance

We mentor learners to build confidence, discipline, leadership skills and strong academic performance for future careers.
