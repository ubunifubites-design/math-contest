Akili Quest Initiative (AQI)

Ubunifu Programs operate under the Akili Quest Initiative (AQI), a learning and mentorship platform committed to empowering learners in Kenya through quality education, innovation and practical skill development. Through AQI, we nurture academic excellence while building strong leadership, teamwork, confidence, creativity and problem-solving abilities that prepare learners for real-world success and future careers.
