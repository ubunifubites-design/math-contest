About Ubunifu

Ubunifu is a learning and excellence organization committed to helping learners succeed in Mathematics, Science, and innovation through competitions, congress programs, mentorship, and hands-on training.

Who We Are

Ubunifu partners with private schools, public schools, and compassion projects to empower learners with academic excellence and 21st century skills. We believe every learner can succeed when given the right environment, guidance, and exposure to competitive learning experiences.

Our Mission

To inspire and empower learners to excel in Mathematics and Science through competitions, mentorship, creativity, and innovation programs that build confidence and future readiness.

Our Vision

To become a leading educational excellence organization that produces learners with strong academic performance and practical 21st century skills for future careers and leadership.

Our Core Values

Excellence, Integrity, Innovation, Teamwork, Discipline, and Learner Empowerment. We focus on creating meaningful programs that bring long-term impact.
